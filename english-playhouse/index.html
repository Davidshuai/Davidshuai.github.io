For grown-ups: tapping Talk starts one short listening turn. Your browser may send speech to its online recognition service. This game does not save recordings. Voice play needs internet access and a supported browser. Tapping always works.

🌱 A little note for grown-ups

Play together for a few happy minutes. Try “More!” and “Here you go!” without asking your child to repeat every word. Help with the microphone, take turns, and celebrate choices. Young voices may not be recognized every time. There are no scores or wrong-answer sounds.
Voice commands: food names, Stir, Here you go, More, All done, Bear, Bunny, Cat. Tap Talk before each turn. The microphone turns off after each turn.
